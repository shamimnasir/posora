must keep a source record here before they are used in a scene.

## Scientific and everyday instruments

These exact-object CC0 models replace generic procedural stand-ins in
Discovery's instruments and inventions collections:

- [Industrial Microscope](https://polyhaven.com/a/industrial_microscope), by
  Lukas Walzer, used for “মাইক্রোস্কোপ”.
- [Seadogs Compass](https://polyhaven.com/a/seadogs_compass), by Benny Weimer,
  used for “কম্পাস”.
- [Alarm Clock 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/alarm_clock_01), modeled by James Ray
  Cock and rigged by Yann Kervran, used for “ঘড়ি”.
- [Korean Public Payphone 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/korean_public_payphone_01),
  by UM JOORIN, used as a historical public-telephone example, not as a modern
  mobile phone.

All are Poly Haven CC0 1.0 models, bundled as optimized GLB files with 1K PBR
textures. Individual records are in their model folders.
