This is a Griffon vulture (`Gyps fulvus`) model used as a high-detail, prey-free body-form comparison for the White-rumped vulture (`Gyps bengalensis`). The two birds share the genus `Gyps`, but this model is not the exact lesson species. Species-identifying plumage, range and conservation facts must be learned from Posora's lesson text and cited references.
